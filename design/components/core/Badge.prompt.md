Status / category label and section eyebrows.

```jsx
<Badge variant="solid">Yeni</Badge>
<Badge variant="onImage">Çok satan</Badge>
<Badge variant="eyebrow">Bu hafta tezgâhta</Badge>
```

`soft` almond pill (default), `solid` filled almond, `onImage` cream pill for placing over product photos, `eyebrow` for uppercase section overlines (almond-600, 0.16em tracking).
